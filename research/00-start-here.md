## The product, in six lines

- **Bellenoor Retinal Shot** — retinaldehyde facial serum, duo set, 2 tubes × 15 ml.
- 0.1% retinaldehyde, encapsulated, delivered by ~200 000 spicules ("mikronålar"), buffered with a 9-peptide complex and panthenol. Fragrance-free.
- Sold at **bellenoor.se**, in Swedish, to Sweden. 349 kr (1-pack) / 529 kr (2-pack) / 698 kr (3-pack + free SPF 50). 60-day money-back guarantee, free shipping.
- Positioned as professional results at home — an alternative to botox, clinics and prescription retinoids.
- Traffic: Meta (Facebook/Instagram) paid ads, cold audiences.
- Live campaign reference: **"rs test"** in the Bellenoor ads account — 2.22 ROAS lifetime.

## The customer, in one line

A Swedish woman, core 43–52, who has a drawer of half-used serums, is afraid of retinol peeling
because she faces people at work, and wants to look **rested** rather than young.

## The strategy, in one line

She has to believe **it was never her fault — nothing she bought ever got past the surface** —
before anything else on the page can work.

## Standing rules for this project

1. **Customer-facing copy is Swedish.** Never write Swedish by translating English marketing
   phrasing — it reads as an imported infomercial and this market punishes that. Write from
   harvested Swedish language.
2. **Tone: low-consciousness structure, high-trust delivery.** Simple language, big emotion, calm
   claims. Swedish consumers read hype as a scam. Honesty about the difficult first two weeks is a
   conversion asset, not a weakness.
3. **No unverified claims.** Anything marked **[VERIFY]** or ⚠️ in these files is not cleared for
   publication. This is a live commercial page in the EU under cosmetics and consumer-protection
   rules — an invented statistic is a legal exposure, not a stylistic choice.
4. **Never present constructed quotes as real customer quotes.** File 01's language sections and
   any example copy are placeholders until the research replaces them.
5. **Every section of copy serves exactly one belief from file 03.** If it serves none, cut it.

## Open decisions blocking the work

These need answers from the founder, not from research:

1. **Are the four named advisors real, contracted people?** (Dr. Anders Lindholm, Linnéa Sjögren,
   Malin Forsberg, Sofia Holmgren.) If not, they come off the page.
2. **How many nights does one tube last?** This blocks the cost-per-night line, which is the
   strongest argument available against the clinic comparison.
3. **What are the actual return mechanics?** Who pays return shipping, is an opened tube accepted,
   how long does a refund take?
4. **Where do the 93 / 89 / 91 / 94% figures come from?**
5. **Is the "two discoveries, two continents" origin story true?** If it isn't the real origin, it
   runs as an ingredient story instead, not a founder story.
